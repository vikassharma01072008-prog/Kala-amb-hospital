Our Journey

Built to Serve. Built for Care.

Kala Amb Multispeciality Hospital was founded in 2026 with one clear goal — to bring dependable, high-quality medical care to a region where families often had to travel hours for basic treatment.

Our hospital is located on Tirlokpur Road in Kala Amb, at the heart of Sirmour district, Himachal Pradesh. We serve families across Kala Amb, Nahan, Paonta Sahib, and the surrounding areas of Himachal Pradesh and southern Haryana.

We started with a simple belief: no patient should have to travel far for quality care. That belief shapes everything we do — from the doctors we bring on board, to the facilities we invest in, to the way we treat every patient who walks through our doors.

Our team of six specialist doctors covers a wide range of medical needs — General Medicine, Surgery, Gynaecology, Urology, Orthopedics, and Pediatrics. Together with a 24x7 emergency unit, in-house diagnostics, pharmacy, and ambulance service, we're equipped to handle everything from routine check-ups to urgent care.
